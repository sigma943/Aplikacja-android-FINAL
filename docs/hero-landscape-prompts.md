# Selected source and extraction prompts

## hero-master

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Source matte-painting asset for an animated realistic mountain landscape app, NOT a phone mockup. Reconstruct the SAME landscape composition from the reference: remove ALL interface, ALL text, buttons, screen/frame/borders. One full-bleed portrait 850x1850 image. Realistic cinematic landscape with highly detailed natural conifer forests, rugged blue-grey mountains and a broad winding river. Camera/framing MUST match reference: sky occupies the upper40%; left high peak around (25%,42%), right high peak around (90%,43%), central low valley skyline around (58%,51%). Distant river first appears at (57%,59%); broad river winds gently across lower40%, around THREE wooded peninsulas. The nearest water takes up most of the lower-right half, with open rippled water from x45% to90% in the bottom quarter. Soft out-of-focus golden-green leaves frame only leftmost20% and a small lower-right edge. No tall foreground tree wall blocking the river. Wide cinematic depth, fine fir silhouettes and soft blue atmospheric layers. Neutral late-afternoon DAYLIGHT materials, blue sky with a few thin layered cumulus/cloud banks near the horizon, plenty of OPEN SKY. Soft neutral water, no bright reflection path. NO sun, NO moon, no shafts, no sunset orange wash, no stars. The engine adds dynamic sky lighting and reflections later. Photographic natural materials, not comic/vector/low-poly, no oversharpening or painterly brush strokes. Preserve the reference's broad elegant river proportions and valley composition; don't invent a triangular zigzag reservoir. No buildings, people, UI or captions.

## hero-terrain

Use case: background-extraction. Extract the TERRAIN ONLY from this exact input landscape. Keep the input camera, composition, every mountain, forest peninsula, individual tree edge, foreground leaf and rock at exactly the SAME pixel position. Same 850x1850 portrait, NO moving/zooming/reframing/cropping or repainting objects. Make ALL SKY and ALL CLOUDS completely transparent alpha. Make EVERY visible WATER/RIVER surface completely transparent alpha, preserving all tree-covered islands, peninsulas and shore silhouettes precisely. Keep the original landscape colours and fine photographic textures. Do not add a water fill, black background, sun, reflections, fog paint, text or UI. Output production terrain cutout: alpha holes for sky and river, opaque forest/mountains/foreground. Especially preserve the wide OPEN river in the lower-right from the input. This is exact layer extraction from this source, not a new scene.

## hero-clouds

Use case: background-extraction. Extract ONLY the clouds from the input master, at the same pixel positions on the same 850x1850 portrait canvas. The master is the camera/layout reference. Keep its fine realistic thin cirrus and horizontally layered small cumulus, with soft translucent edges and neutral cream/white/grey detail. Make ALL blue sky, ALL mountains, forest, foliage and river transparent alpha. Nothing except clouds should remain. Do not move, zoom, recolour, enlarge or add large towering cloud masses. Keep the open central sky clear, and keep all lower60% fully transparent. No sun, moon, stars, text, frame or UI. Actual alpha transparency, no checkerboard or coloured background.
